# PC Plus+ Changelog

All notable changes to PC Plus+ are listed here.

## 1.0.6 – The Group Update
- **See other PC Plus+ users:** a green `+` now appears next to the name of every other PC Plus+ user in the multiplayer tab list.
- **In-world marker:** the same green `+` floats above a player's head when you walk near them.
- **Works on any server:** the `+` marker shows up on every multiplayer server you join — no server-side install required.

## 1.0.5 – The Favorites & IVs Update
- **Favorite system:** hover over a Pokémon and press `Ctrl + R` to favorite it. Favorites get a yellow star (★) and are protected from the "Trash Selected" button.
- **`@favorite` filter button** to instantly show only your favorited Pokémon.
- **IV filtering dropdown:** filter your PC by 70%+, 80%+, 90%+, or perfect 100% IVs.
- **GUI overhaul:** Types and IV menus rescaled, aligned with the search bar, and moved clear of the trash can.

## Earlier releases – Core features
- **Filter chaining / stackable searches:** combine tag buttons with the Types dropdown (e.g. a Shiny Water-type, or a Dragon & Flying Legendary).
- **Dual-type filtering dropdown:** scroll all 18 types and select up to two at once.
- **Custom UI buttons:** `@shiny`, `@legendary`, `@mythical`, `@ultrabeast`, `@HA`, `@textured`, and `L100`.
- **Level 100 filter** for max-level, battle-ready Pokémon.
- **Accurate Hidden Ability detection** via the `@HA` button.
- **Full Gen 1 – Gen 9 support**, including Paldean and Paradox Pokémon.
- **Ctrl-click multi-move** to select and move multiple Pokémon at once.
- **Mass delete (Trash button)** to clear out breedjects in seconds.
- **Valuables protection:** safety scanner blocks accidental deletion of Favorites, Legendaries, Mythicals, Ultra Beasts, HAs, 100 IVs, and Textured Pokémon.
- **Custom keybinds** for the Multi-Select key.
- **One-click "Clear All"** to reset your view.

**Requirements:** Minecraft 1.16.5 · Forge 36.2.34+ · Pixelmon 9.1.13+
